# Bill Planner Support

Need help with Bill Planner? Have a suggestion? We’d love to hear from you.

## Contact Us

Email: clear-bill-planner@outlook.com

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, please contact us at the address above.

## Reporting a Problem

To help us understand the issue, please include:

- Your device model and iOS version
- Your Bill Planner app version
- What happened and what you expected
- Steps to reproduce the issue, if possible

Screenshots can be helpful. Please hide any personal or financial information before sharing them. Never send passwords or Apple Account credentials.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to connect a bank account?

No. Bill Planner lets you record income and expenses and plan upcoming bills without connecting a bank account or maintaining account balances.

### Can I use Bill Planner offline?

Yes. You can record and review your bills offline. An internet connection is needed to sync changes through iCloud.

### What should I check if iCloud isn’t syncing?

Make sure your devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud is enabled for Bill Planner, and you have available iCloud storage. Connect your devices to the internet and allow time for syncing.

If the issue continues, contact us before deleting the app or its iCloud data.

### Can you access my financial records?

We do not have access to the records stored on your device or in your private iCloud database. Please describe the issue without sending your full financial records.

### How can I suggest a feature?

Email us with your idea and a brief explanation of how it would help you use Bill Planner.
